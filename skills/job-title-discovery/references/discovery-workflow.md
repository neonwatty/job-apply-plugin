# Browser research and privacy

Use the owner's current resume title and relevant skills as context, alongside saved `targetTitles` and explicitly supplied role interests, seeds, seniority limits, and exclusions. A target may differ from the resume title. Do not edit the factual profile or assume a higher level from tenure alone.

Search LinkedIn Jobs in the host-managed visible browser with the smallest useful set of role queries. Inspect result titles and job detail pages when accessible; record exact job URLs, observed titles, and company names only when visible. A search page, snippet, or stale listing is not proof of an employer, salary, date, or seniority beyond what it shows. Do not submit applications or access account settings. Use the owner's existing browser session if available; never request, copy, or store LinkedIn credentials.

Use observed title vocabulary to propose a small, useful set: **core** roles closely matching demonstrated work, **adjacent** roles with transferable work and a clear gap, and **stretch** roles requiring a stated transition or expanded scope. Explain the link to the person's context and cite each recommendation with at least one observed job URL. Keep different role families and levels distinct. Collapse only cosmetic duplicates such as case or whitespace variants. Honor exclusions and avoid claims about compensation, hiring likelihood, or qualifications not supported by the source.

If LinkedIn requires login, is blocked, fails to load, or yields no useful title evidence, set the corresponding source status in the packet and return no evidence-backed suggestions. Explain the limitation in `source.detail`. Offer a browser retry after owner action or a user-guided continuation with new seed titles; identify any brainstorming outside the packet as unverified. Never label an unvisited or inaccessible page as searched. Other requested job boards can be researched only with the same visible evidence standard.

Keep the packet limited to title recommendations and public source metadata. Do not include resume text, private profile facts, email, credentials, or raw browser page dumps. Do not transmit the resume or profile to a search form or external service; queries should contain role words chosen or approved for research. Do not store research in the canonical Store. Return the packet for Companion review, where cancel and error paths make no write.
