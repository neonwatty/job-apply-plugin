# Job Apply v1.3.10

- On Windows x64, you can now check a legacy Store and migrate it to a separate native Store without Python. Rollback creates a separate native recovery copy from the verified legacy data and preserves newer edits in the migrated Store. [Migration and rollback guide](https://github.com/neonwatty/job-apply-plugin/blob/v1.3.10/docs/runtime-support.md#explicit-windows-store-migration)
- Companion keeps browser handoffs in the correct Needs Attention filter, lets you record a manual submission after the agent releases its claim, and retries résumé extraction status after a failed facts request.
- The Job Apply website has a refreshed landing page, updated Companion branding, and a new social preview card.
