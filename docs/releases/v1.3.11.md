# Job Apply v1.3.11

- **Submit after approval** is available in Companion Settings and starts off. Enable it to let the agent submit an exact reviewed application or named batch after you explicitly approve it.
- When Chrome masks a required value such as a populated email address or phone number, the agent leaves the draft at Review so you can submit it yourself.
- Companion shows confirmed submission receipts in Job Activity and flags uncertain outcomes for verification. You can transfer a confirmed receipt from an isolated Store to the matching saved job in another Store.
- The Job Apply website has updated onboarding, supported-platform logos, and examples of reused application answers.
